Meet Julio Rocha

His Mission

Why I'm Running

I've spent my life helping families in District 8 make tough financial decisions—buying a first home, protecting a small business, planning for retirement. I see the same challenges over and over again: housing that's out of reach, roads that steal hours from our day, and jobs that don't keep up with the cost of living.

I'm running for County Commission because we don't need more political speeches. We need a commissioner who understands budgets, listens for a living, and knows how to turn plans into results. That's what I've done for 26 years in business, and that's exactly how I'll serve District 8.

What We'll Do Together

Priorities for District 8

Housing You Can Actually Afford

  • Fast-track projects that include real workforce and affordable housing.
  • Create down-payment assistance so teachers, nurses, and first responders can buy in the communities they serve.
  • Protect longtime homeowners and seniors from being priced out of their neighborhoods.

Less Time in Traffic, More Time at Home

  • Improve bus frequency and reliability on the routes District 8 families actually use.
  • Push for smarter traffic flow, better road maintenance, and safer intersections.
  • Invest in walkable, bikeable streets so kids and seniors can move safely.

Real Opportunity for Working Families

  • Cut red tape for small businesses and make it easier to start and grow in District 8.
  • Partner with local colleges and employers on job training that leads to higher-wage careers.
  • Prioritize jobs that offer a living wage and a path to the middle class.
