About the Candidate

Meet Julio Rocha, MBA

For 26 years, Julio has helped Orange County families protect what they've earned and build the future they deserve. As co-owner of Rocha Financial Group, he's sat across the table from hundreds of neighbors facing rising housing costs, long commutes, and economic uncertainty—and helped them find real answers.

A trusted financial professional with an MBA, Julio brings real-world business expertise—not political talking points—to every challenge District 8 faces.

  • 26 years serving Orange County families as a small business owner
  • Co-owner, Rocha Financial Group – insurance and financial planning
  • Former treasurer for Congresswoman Val Demings' first campaign
  • Chair, Hispanic Outreach Committee, Orange County Democratic Executive Committee

His Mission

Why I'm Running

I've spent my life helping families in District 8 make tough financial decisions—buying a first home, protecting a small business, planning for retirement. I see the same challenges over and over: housing that's out of reach, roads that steal hours from our day, and jobs that don't keep up with the cost of living.

I'm running because we need a commissioner who understands budgets, listens for a living, and turns plans into results. That's what I've done for 26 years in business.

What We'll Do Together

Priorities for District 8

Three focused commitments to make District 8 a place everyone can afford to call home.

Housing You Can Actually Afford

  • Fast-track projects that include real workforce and affordable housing.
  • Create down-payment assistance so teachers, nurses, and first responders can buy in the communities they serve.
  • Protect longtime homeowners and seniors from being priced out.

Less Time in Traffic, More Time at Home

  • Improve bus frequency and reliability on routes District 8 families actually use.
  • Push for smarter traffic flow, better road maintenance, and safer intersections.
  • Invest in walkable, bikeable streets for kids and seniors.

Real Opportunity for Working Families

  • Cut red tape for small businesses and make it easier to start and grow.
  • Partner with local colleges and employers on job training for higher-wage careers.
  • Prioritize jobs that offer a living wage and a path to the middle class.
